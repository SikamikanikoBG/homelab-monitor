🚨 Anomalies

Latest GPU & power readings scored against their own recent baseline (rolling mean ± stddev). A flag means the value is far enough from normal to be worth a look — flat or idle series never raise one.

🛟 Incidents

Anomalies that fire together are grouped into one correlated incident with a lifecycle — opening when the cluster trips and clearing once every member has settled back to normal. One incident, many signals.

🧠 VRAM headroom & ETA

Free VRAM is total minus what loaded models currently hold. The ETA fits VRAM-used over the last few hours; it only shows a time-to-full when the trend is credibly rising under load, otherwise it reads “stable”.

🧠 LLM engine

Real throughput from this host's local LLM — tokens/sec and time-to-first-token measured off the Copilot's own generations (no cloud, no synthetic numbers), plus the models resident in VRAM right now with their GPU/CPU split and keep-alive countdown.

🔀 Notification routing

Send matching alerts to a specific channel instead of every channel. A route matches when the alert's subject (container/service/host/series/mount/check name) fits its pattern and its level is at or above the route's minimum. Routes are checked top-to-bottom; an alert goes to every matching route's channel.

No routes = every alert goes to its rule's own channel (today's default). Routing only ever redirects matching alerts — it never drops them.

⬆ Docker image-update awareness

Compares each running container's deployed image digest with the upstream registry's current tag digest (Docker Hub, GHCR, and other public v2 registries) and flags when a newer image exists. Awareness only — it NEVER pulls, runs, restarts or deletes anything. Heavily cached and bounded; off by default to avoid background registry traffic. Private/digest-pinned/local-only images show as "unknown".

💾 Backup & Restore

Export your SQLite history, events, alert settings, and host registry — or restore a snapshot after a container recreate or disk move. SSH keys under /data/.ssh are not included; back those up separately if needed.
